Sources: The Walt Disney Company Annual Reports (Form 10-K) for fiscal {{YEARS_DESC_LIST}} — consolidated statements of income, cash flows and equity, segment information notes (including capital expenditures by segment), revenue notes and MD&A segment and liquidity tables. Each year's figures are sourced from that year's report, with later reports' comparative columns as alternates; the segment-expense categories come from the first report that disclosed them (the 2025 report, for all three of its years). Capital-expenditure blocks also list the ships and park projects the reports name, each linked to its passage. The Consumer Products reporting change is from the fiscal 2026 third-quarter earnings release (SEC Form 8-K, Exhibit 99.1). All figures in U.S. dollars, millions, as reported.

Clicking a block opens the page of the report that the figure comes from, with the figure highlighted; where a figure also appears in a later report's comparative column, that page is offered as an alternate.

Segment revenues are shown gross; intersegment fees removed in consolidation flow to their own block so the segments add up to consolidated revenue. Segment P&Ls use the expense categories Disney reports for each segment; segment operating income is before corporate costs, acquisition amortization and restructuring.
